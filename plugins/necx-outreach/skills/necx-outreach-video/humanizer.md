# Humanizer pass for NECX emails

Apply to every subject and body after drafting. When a `humanizer` skill is installed, run it first, then check this list. The tools reject the hard rules (marked ✗); the rest is judgment.

## Hard rules (rejected by the tools)
- ✗ **AI-stock words:** delve, tapestry, testament to, pivotal, seamless, robust, leverage, elevate, empower, unlock, game-changing, cutting-edge, revolutionize, transformative, synergy, holistic, landscape, realm, embark, streamline, harness, crucial, ever-evolving.
- ✗ **Filler transitions:** Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, In conclusion, It's worth noting.
- ✗ **Sales clichés:** hope this email finds you well, I wanted to reach out, touch base, circle back, just following up, quick call, perfect fit, don't hesitate, rest assured, at the end of the day, I'm excited to, please find.
- ✗ **Contrast tricks:** "not only X but also Y", "it's not just X, it's Y".
- ✗ **Punctuation:** em dashes and en dashes (use a comma, period or parentheses), exclamation marks, emoji.
- ✗ **ALL-CAPS words** (funder acronyms like VOCA and FVPSA are fine).
- ✗ **Links in the text.** The video is the only link, placed with a `[VIDEO]` line.
- ✗ **Length:** video/text emails over about 140 words, bumps over about 70, break-ups over about 90. Subjects over 10 words.

## Judgment
- **Write like one person writing to another.** Short sentences, everyday words, contractions.
- **One pain, one proof, one question.** Cut everything else.
- **No staged openers** ("Picture this", "Imagine if", "Here's the thing").
- **No forced triads** ("faster, smarter, better") unless the three items are real.
- **No inflated claims.** Say what NECX does, using the verified catalog wording.
- **No one-line closers that restate the pitch.** End on the question.
- **Use their words:** their program names, their funders, their agency name, as written on their site.
- **Subject lines:** specific and plain ("VOCA and FVPSA, counted once"), never clickbait or a question stack.
- **Bumps are one or two sentences,** and offer a way out ("If this sits with someone else, a name is all I need").
- **Break-ups are kind and final.** No guilt, no "did I lose you".
- **Read it aloud.** If a sentence sounds like a brochure, rewrite it the way you'd say it on the phone.
